Domain Warped Noise

Grey-tone noise built by warping one turbulence field with another (feDisplacementMap), twice, then modulating the result with a third field and folding it through a transfer curve. Surface stages (weave, grain, flecks, relief, edge grime) sit on top. Everything stays greyscale — the luma is pushed into all three channels after each mixing stage. Presets are also linkable: ?preset=canvas-scan.
